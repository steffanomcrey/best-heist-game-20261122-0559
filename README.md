**Copper Fox: The Meridian Job** — a top-down night heist in three levels. Slip through the Gallery, crack the Vault Hall for the Meridian Diamond, and reach the rooftop helicopter. Guards patrol with raycast vision cones, CCTV cameras sweep the rooms, laser grids blink on a cycle, and shadow pools hide you. Smoke, EMP and coin lures cost alarm. Grab every target, keep the alarm under 100, and get out clean.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
# open http://localhost:8080
```

WASD or arrows to move (or click the floor to walk there), 1 smoke, 2 EMP, 3 coin lure, P pause, R restart, Enter to start. No network, no build step, plain static files.
